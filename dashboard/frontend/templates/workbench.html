    A Fair Value Gap is a 3-candle pattern where the middle candle moves so aggressively that it creates a gap between the first and third candles' wicks. This gap represents an area where price moved too fast for fair value to be established. Price often returns to fill these gaps. Bullish FVG (green) = gap below price, potential support. Bearish FVG (red) = gap above price, potential resistance.

    FVG (Fair Value Gap)
    A 3-candle imbalance zone. Institutions often return to fill these.
    Bullish FVG
    Gap below price (candle 3 low > candle 1 high). Potential buying zone.
    Bearish FVG
    Gap above price (candle 1 low > candle 3 high). Potential selling zone.
    Filled Gap
    An FVG that price has since traded through, invalidating the zone.
