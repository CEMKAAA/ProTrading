# Preset Layout

## MT4 Bridge Presets

- `presets/Winners/Atomic/Normal`: standard Atomic bridge presets
- `presets/Winners/Atomic/FTMO`: Atomic presets with advanced risk management enabled
- `presets/Winners/Atomic/Tools`: scanner and diagnostic presets
- `presets/Winners/SIGNATICS/Normal`: supported SIGNATICS buffer presets
- `presets/Winners/SIGNATICS/FTMO`: SIGNATICS buffer presets with advanced risk management enabled
- `presets/Winners/SIGNATICS/Indicator`: visual indicator presets
- `presets/Winners/SIGNATICS/Legacy/Normal`: retained standard chart-object and logging presets
- `presets/Winners/SIGNATICS/Legacy/FTMO`: retained FTMO chart-object and logging presets
- `presets/Winners/SIGNATICS/Tools`: scanner and diagnostic presets

## MT5 NAS Presets

- `EaPresets/NAS/V1/Normal`: standard NAS V1 presets
- `EaPresets/NAS/V1/FTMO`: NAS V1 presets with advanced risk management enabled
- `EaPresets/NAS/V2/Backtest`: NAS V2 Strategy Tester presets
- `EaPresets/NAS/V2/Normal`: standard NAS V2 live presets
- `EaPresets/NAS/V2/FTMO`: NAS V2 live presets with advanced risk management enabled
- `EaPresets/NAS/V3/Normal`: standard NAS V3 live presets
- `EaPresets/NAS/V3/FTMO`: NAS V3 presets with full FTMO daily/max drawdown governor enabled
- `EaPresets/NAS/V3/FTMO/RiskLotOnly`: NAS V3 presets with risk-based lot sizing enabled and daily/max drawdown disabled
- `EaPresets/NAS/V3/Winners/FTMO`: generated NAS V3 winner presets from internal 20-week backtests, full FTMO governor enabled
- `EaPresets/NAS/V3/Winners/RiskLotOnly`: generated NAS V3 winner presets from internal 20-week backtests, risk-lot only

## MT5 ORB Presets

- `EaPresets/ORB15/Normal`: standard ORB presets
- `EaPresets/ORB15/FTMO`: ORB presets with full FTMO daily/max drawdown governor enabled
- `EaPresets/ORB15/FTMO/RiskLotOnly`: ORB presets with risk-based lot sizing enabled and daily/max drawdown disabled
- `EaPresets/ORB15/Winners/FTMO`: generated ORB winner presets from internal 20-week backtests, full FTMO governor enabled
- `EaPresets/ORB15/Winners/RiskLotOnly`: generated ORB winner presets from internal 20-week backtests, risk-lot only

## MT5 ERL/IRL Presets

- `EaPresets/ERL_IRL/Winners/FTMO`: generated ERL/IRL winner presets from internal 20-week backtests, full FTMO governor enabled
- `EaPresets/ERL_IRL/Winners/RiskLotOnly`: generated ERL/IRL winner presets from internal 20-week backtests, risk-lot only

The active MetaTrader preset folders mirror this structure.
